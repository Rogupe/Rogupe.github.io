About

I'm Rodrigo — a mechatronics engineer finishing two degrees at once: B.Sc. Mechatronics at Tecnológico de Monterrey and an M.Sc. in Logistics & Supply Chain Management at the University of Hull (Course Representative, Certificate of Recognition).

On the hardware side: a 3D-scanning quality-control rig at Magna CIMS, RGB-D vision research at CINVESTAV, and Design Lead of Team Romeo's XPRIZE wildfire drones at GTI — four flying prototypes and an ExpoIngenierías technical excellence award. On the data side: my MSc dissertation builds an AI-integrated digital twin for an automotive Tier 1 export supply chain (GRU forecasting + NSGA-II optimisation, on 500+ real order records).

I learned German to B2 in six months — then taught it. I like hard problems and short deadlines.
